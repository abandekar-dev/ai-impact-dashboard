Question 1: Where will AI create real business value in the next 12 months?

Sub-Questions:

Which parts of our value chain are bottlenecked by human throughput?

What tasks consume high effort but low judgment?

Where do we have rich data but under-leveraged insight?

Which customer touchpoints can be made more proactive or predictive?

What high-frequency workflows are most repeatable across teams?

Question 2: Are we building AI into the business — or just layering it on top?

Sub-Questions:

Which teams use AI in a meaningful, embedded way vs. tool-based add-ons?

What % of workflows have AI involved in core decision points?

Where does human-AI collaboration feel clunky, redundant, or untrusted?

What work would break if we turned off the AI layer?

What process redesigns are required to move from augmentation to integration?

Question 3: Do we have the talent — not just technical, but managerial — to run an AI-augmented business?

Sub-Questions:

Which critical roles have high exposure to AI but low readiness?

Who in our leadership team can speak fluently about AI trade-offs?

Are we developing 'AI adjacent' capabilities like critical thinking, prompt design, and ethical oversight?

Which teams are asking smart questions about AI, not just using it?

Where is our reskill vs. hire decision most urgent?

Question 4: What risks are we not seeing — and what’s our model for responsible AI use?

Sub-Questions:

Where in our workflows do AI models make or influence consequential decisions?

Which outputs are currently unexplainable to a human reviewer?

What data sources could introduce bias or hallucination risk?

What’s our human override process — and who owns it?

How do we test trust with frontline users before full-scale deployment?

Question 5: What are we doing now that will give us an AI advantage three years from now?

Sub-Questions:

What proprietary data or workflows are we capturing today that no one else is?

How are we turning user interactions into compounding model learning?

Where are we building internal AI fluency — not just capability?

What are we building that will be harder to replicate than a model?

Are we designing systems to get smarter the more we use them?
